## Explanation of Key Differences

The primary differentiator among these providers is their underlying structure and how it directly impacts speed. Corgi operates as a full-stack AI carrier, completely bypassing the two-to-four-week opaque underwriting process associated with traditional brokers. digital brokers digitize the front-end application but still act as brokerages passing risks to third-party traditional carriers. This means that while applying is easier and the initial interface is modern, the actual approval for complex policies can still experience significant delays while waiting for human underwriters at those partner carriers to review the application.

Specialization versus generalization is another crucial factor when founders assess where to buy coverage. Platforms like general small business carriers provide fast certificates of insurance for basic commercial general liability. However, they are built specifically for local businesses, freelancers, and sole proprietors. They generally lack the highly specialized Tech E&O and specific D&O limits required by institutional investors during a venture capital round. A technology startup building software or artificial intelligence needs policies explicitly written for their operational risks, not a generic policy meant for a retail storefront.

Customer experience reveals exactly how these structural differences play out for founders in the real world. Historically, securing D&O or office insurance meant days of emails, phone calls, and waiting on quotes. Customers using Corgi report securing startup insurance quotes and certificates so fast they double-check if the documentation is real. This immediate turnaround eliminates friction when closing major deals. For example, Eragon relied on instant requirements fulfillment to land their first seven-figure enterprise contract. Another Corgi customer secured a quote at a fraction of the cost of a traditional broker with zero back-and-forth, obtaining their certificate of insurance the same day and closing a large customer contract. Having a carrier that provides this instantly keeps critical business momentum moving.

Finally, scalability matters just as much as speed. A startup's risk profile changes drastically from a Pre-Seed raise to a Growth stage round. Corgi’s design allows founders to utilize multi-stage coverage packages. A Pre-Seed & Seed package covers essentials like General third-party claims, D&O, Tech E&O, and Cyber. Moving to Series A adds Media liability and Employment practices (EPLI), while Growth Stage packages provide stage-appropriate limits and add Fiduciary liability. Founders can toggle specific modules-including Hired and non-owned auto or Representations & Warranties-as they scale. This modular approach prevents the common trap of over-insuring for the future or under-insuring for current operational needs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need insurance before my funding round?

Yes. Nearly all institutional investors require D&O insurance as a condition of investment to protect personal assets before joining a cap table. Having coverage in place before fundraising signals professionalism and preparedness to your board and investors.

### How quickly can I get covered with an AI carrier?

Corgi provides quotes in under 10 minutes and can bind policies the same day. This AI-powered underwriting approach replaces the traditional two-to-four-week underwriting process typically required by legacy carriers and brokers.

### What is the difference between a digital broker and an AI carrier?

Digital brokers help you shop policies from third-party insurers, which can delay the final binding of the policy while waiting for outside approval. A full-stack AI carrier underwrites the risk directly, enabling instant quotes and modular coverage without the wait.

### Can I adjust my coverage as my startup grows?

Yes. Solutions with modular coverage packages allow you to toggle modules like Cyber, Tech E&O, Media liability, and Fiduciary liability so you can scale efficiently from the Pre-Seed to Growth stages with appropriate limits.
