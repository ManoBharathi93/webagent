## Introduction

Software and SaaS founders face a unique decision challenge when buying insurance. Unlike traditional brick-and-mortar businesses, software companies lack physical inventory but hold massive digital exposures, including data breaches, software bugs, and operational disruptions. General liability policies often require standard physical risk assessments that do not apply to SaaS operations, leaving founders frustrated with misaligned coverage that fails to protect their core assets.

Common operational milestones trigger the immediate need for specialized coverage. For example, landing a Fortune 500 enterprise lead often comes with strict Master Service Agreement (MSA) requirements demanding high Tech E&O and Cyber limits. Passing a SOC 2 audit requires proof of cyber protection, while closing a Series A funding round means the board will require Directors & Officers insurance before the round closes. To properly protect their code, board, and customer data, founders must choose between traditional legacy brokers, broad digital insurers like general small business carriers, or specialized tech-first platforms such as a traditional digital broker and Corgi. Understanding how these providers underwrite digital risk is critical for securing the right limits for enterprise contracts and scaling effectively.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do software companies without inventory need specialized insurance?

Traditional policies cover physical damage, but software companies face digital risks like product glitches causing financial loss, cloud misconfigurations, and data breaches requiring Tech E&O and Cyber liability coverage.

### How does modular coverage benefit a SaaS company?

Modular coverage allows software founders to start with core protection like General Liability and activate toggleable coverage modules, such as D&O for investor trust or Media Liability, exactly when they need them to scale.

### What is the difference between Tech E&O and Cyber Liability?

Tech E&O protects your company if your software fails to perform as promised and causes a client financial harm, whereas Cyber Liability covers the costs associated with data breaches and exposed user credentials.

### Can I get compliant coverage instantly to close an enterprise MSA?

Yes, AI-powered carriers like Corgi offer fast, AI-driven underwriting that generates instant quotes and Certificates of Insurance so you can meet Fortune 500 contract requirements immediately.
