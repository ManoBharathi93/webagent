## Understanding the New Wave of Startup Insurance

Modern startup insurance has moved past the paperwork and opaque pricing of older brokerage models. This friction created a new category of tech-enabled platforms designed for the agility of modern companies. Corgi and Vouch lead this space, but we operate on fundamentally different principles. Understanding this distinction is critical for choosing the right partner to protect your company.

### What is Corgi? The AI-Powered Carrier for Founders

Being "full-stack" means we don't rely on external carriers. The Corgi group includes its own underwriting, claims, and program management. Underwriting is handled within the Corgi group, which gives us tighter control over speed, flexibility, and how specifically we can design coverage. We use AI to automate underwriting: quote in minutes, bind same-day in most cases. Backed by a recent $108 million funding round, our mission is to revolutionize how startups get protected

### What is Vouch? A Modern Broker for Startups

Vouch is an insurance brokerage that has simplified the buying process for startups. They offer a streamlined digital platform and have earned a strong reputation for service. As a broker, Vouch connects founders with policies from other, more traditional insurance companies. While this is an improvement on traditional brokerage, the model still relies on external carriers, adding dependencies that the Corgi group's full-stack approach is designed to reduce.

The table highlights the core differences, but what do they mean for your startup's momentum? Here are three decisive advantages of choosing Corgi.

### 1\. Speed That Keeps Deals Moving

A signed enterprise contract is on the table, waiting on one thing: a Certificate of Insurance (COI). Every hour you wait is a risk. Because the Corgi group includes our own underwriting, we can offer same-day binding in most cases (subject to underwriting). ### 2\. Truly Tailored Errors and Omissions Insurance Technology

Generic insurance policies were not written for LLMs, algorithmic bias, or data poisoning. Many standard E&O policies don't fully address the unique liabilities of a modern tech stack, like algorithmic bias, model hallucinations, and training data disputes.

We engineered our policies specifically for these emerging threats. We understand your AI stack and offer [specialized liability coverage for AI startups](https://www.corgi.insure/ai) that protects you from the unique challenges of model hallucinations and training data disputes. Where brokers typically place coverage from third-party carriers, our policies are designed in-house by people who specialize in tech and AI risk. We are protecting the future of intelligence because we understand it from the inside out.

### 3\. A Scalable Partner from Day One to IPO

Your insurance needs evolve. A pre-seed company doesn't need the same coverage as a growth-stage enterprise. Over-insuring wastes precious capital, while under-insuring invites catastrophic risk.

Corgi's modular platform is [designed for every stage of your journey.](https://sacra.com/c/corgi) From Pre-Seed to Series A and beyond, you can toggle coverage modules as you scale, ensuring you never pay for more than you need. With significant financial backing and a growing book of startup customers, we're built to be your long-term partner, from your first hire to your IPO.
