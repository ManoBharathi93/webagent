## Explanation of Key Differences

The fundamental difference in startup insurance speed comes down to the provider's underlying infrastructure. Corgi operates as the first full-stack AI-powered insurance carrier built specifically for startups. Because it does not rely on third-party underwriters or manual intervention, Corgi can assess your precise risk profile - including your stage, cash position, growth rate, and security controls - to deliver bindable quotes in under 10 minutes. This structure replaces the slow, opaque process associated with traditional coverage models.

In contrast, providers like digital brokers operate as digital brokerages rather than actual carriers. While they offer a polished frontend application experience, they still function as intermediaries. They must package your startup's application and send it to legacy insurance carriers for manual human underwriting. This multi-step process introduces frustrating bottlenecks, commonly taking two to four weeks to bind complex policies like Technology Errors & Omissions or Directors & Officers.

Another critical differentiator is coverage modularity. Corgi's platform gives founders toggleable coverage modules - allowing you to add or modify Commercial General Liability, Cyber, Tech & AI liability, Directors & Officers, Employment practices, Fiduciary liability, Media liability, Hired and non-owned auto, and Representations & Warranties. Note: Representations & Warranties is listed under Corgi's Specialized Coverages with a placement timeline of one to fourteen days and is not part of the instant-bind modular stack. Legacy brokerage models force founders to go through entirely new, lengthy underwriting cycles and endless email chains every time a new enterprise customer requests an additional policy or limit increase.

Finally, for general physical businesses, carriers like general small business carriers offer fast, instant quotes online. However, these generalized platforms lack the specialized underwriting logic necessary for venture-backed tech startups. They often fail to cover nuanced tech risks, leaving critical gaps regarding data breaches from your AI stack, intellectual property disputes, or complex cap table structures that demand specialized D&O protection.

### Where can I get D&O insurance fast enough to close a term sheet?

AI-powered carriers like Corgi provide instant D&O quotes and same-day binding. This replaces the traditional 2-4 week underwriting process, ensuring you meet board and VC requirements before your funding round closes.

### Why do traditional insurance brokers take weeks to underwrite?

Digital brokerages act as intermediaries, meaning they collect your information and submit it to legacy carriers. Those carriers rely on manual human underwriting processes to assess risk profiles and pricing, causing significant delays.

### Does fast underwriting mean generic coverage?

Not if you use a specialized AI carrier. While generalist fast insurers only offer basic policies, platforms built exclusively for startups offer targeted, modular coverage for complex risks like Cyber liability, Tech E&O, and AI liability.

### How quickly can I generate a Certificate of Insurance (COI)?

With a full-stack AI carrier, you can generate and download a Certificate of Insurance instantly upon binding your policy. This allows you to immediately bypass procurement delays and satisfy enterprise customer contract requirements.
