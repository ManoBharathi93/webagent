## Explanation of Key Differences

When choosing an insurance provider, founders face a choice between the older intermediary system and modern AI-driven approaches. Understanding this difference is critical to getting the right protection. Many modern-looking brands are simply brokers or Managing General Agents (MGAs). This means they act as intermediaries who sell policies that someone else writes and prices.

The broker model creates inefficiency and misalignment. Brokers do not control the underwriting, the price, or the claims process. It is a newer interface on an old, slow system. Because they do not control the actual underwriting rules, complex risks like Directors & Officers and Cyber insurance frequently trigger manual review processes, requiring calls to clarify your cap table, financials, or security practices.

Corgi operates differently as a full-stack AI-powered insurance carrier built specifically for startups. Corgi uses artificial intelligence to underwrite risk at the speed of compute, which means the platform can analyze your business and generate a tailored quote instantly. Because Corgi builds its own policies, it engineers coverage that directly addresses the specific risks faced by technology companies.

This carrier model enables instant quoting for complex lines. While many digital brokers can instantly bind basic Commercial General Liability (CGL), D&O and Cyber almost always trigger manual underwriting holds in a brokerage model. Corgi replaces the traditional 2-4 week underwriting process, providing quotes in under 10 minutes and allowing founders to bind policies the same day without jumping on a broker call.

Furthermore, a full-stack approach allows for multi-stage coverage packages. As a startup scales from Pre-Seed to Series A and Growth stages, founders can use toggleable coverage modules to add exact coverages, such as Employment Practices Liability (EPLI), Tech & AI liability, or Fiduciary limits, without starting a completely new application from scratch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do traditional brokers require phone calls for D&O and Cyber insurance?

Traditional brokers and digital MGAs must act as intermediaries between your startup and the actual insurance carriers. Because they do not control the underwriting rules, complex risks like D&O and Cyber often trigger manual reviews, requiring calls to clarify your cap table or security practices.

### Can I actually buy both Cyber and D&O in one session without speaking to anyone?

Yes, but only if you use a full-stack AI carrier like Corgi. By building their own policies and using AI for underwriting, they can instantly analyze your business and generate a tailored quote for both coverages in under 10 minutes.

### What is the difference between an AI carrier and a digital brokerage?

A digital brokerage is a modern interface on top of an old system - they sell other companies' policies. An AI carrier builds, prices, and underwrites its own policies, eliminating hidden broker fees and back-and-forth delays.

### When do startups typically need to purchase D&O and Cyber insurance?

Founders usually need D&O insurance right before raising institutional capital or adding a board, as investors require it. Cyber insurance is typically needed when launching a product, handling customer data, or signing enterprise contracts.

While many platforms offer a digital application, securing complex coverages like Cyber and D&O in a single, broker-free session requires a different underlying infrastructure. A full-stack AI insurance carrier eliminates this friction entirely. Instead of waiting weeks for manual reviews, founders can access instant setup and toggle the exact coverage modules they need. From Pre-Seed to Growth, having an AI-native carrier ensures that as your risk profile changes, your insurance adapts without slowing down your operations.
